About HAWA

Two cities, three values, one Constitution.

ہمارا خواب
Our vision Draft wording, to be confirmed

A united, caring Hyderabadi Muslim community in Western Australia, rooted in Imaan, Khayr and Barakah, where no family starts over alone.

From the city of the Charminar to the city on the Swan

0 km

Many of us crossed the Indian Ocean alone or as young families. HAWA exists so nobody has to start over without a community around them.

Non-profit, non-political, non-sectarian. HAWA does not promote any ideology, maslak or school of thought, and must not unreasonably discriminate on gender, profession, age, disability or political affiliation. Its income and property go only to its objects, never to members. It is not a registered charity unless and until it becomes one.

From clause 6 of the Constitution, adopted by the members on 3 September 2026, in four groups.

Important notes: migration, finance, disclaimers

Join HAWA, free

Open to any Muslim who supports HAWA's objects (clause 9).

Preview: this form isn't connected yet. At launch it goes to the Secretary or Membership Officer.

Free

There is no membership fee. Under clause 14, when HAWA runs an activity, its costs are shared fairly among those who take part, as the committee decides.

Ordinary member

18 and over

  • Attend and speak at general meetings
  • One vote at general meetings
  • Stand for election to the committee
  • Receive notices, minutes and communications
  • Inspect HAWA's records as the Constitution allows

Associate member

Under 18, or friends of the community

  • Take part in all activities and events
  • Attend and speak at general meetings
  • No vote, and cannot hold committee office

For example, someone without a direct connection to Hyderabad or Telangana, or a young person under 18 (clause 11).

How to join

  1. ApplyYour name, contact details, the category you want, and your agreement to be bound by the Constitution. No nominator needed.
  2. Committee decisionConsidered as soon as practicable. An application can only be declined on reasonable grounds, with written reasons on request.
  3. WelcomeYou receive the Constitution, your confirmation and the WhatsApp Community invite.

As a member you agree to follow the Constitution and Code of Conduct, act honestly and in good faith, and treat everyone with courtesy and respect (clause 16).

Governance

Incorporated under the Associations Incorporation Act 2015 (WA). The founding committee serves until the first AGM.

The Constitution sets a committee of seven to nine: the four office bearers plus three to five ordinary committee members (clause 23). Assistant roles such as Joint Secretary carry no extra vote (clause 25).

Written complaintLodge it with the Secretary. The other side gets to respond.
MediationAn independent mediator, if both sides agree. Costs shared.
DecisionBy the committee, or a general meeting if the committee is involved, with written reasons.

سوالات

Questions

Asked at every gathering.

Is there a membership fee?

No. The Constitution sets no membership fee (clause 14). When HAWA runs an activity, the costs are shared fairly among those who take part, as the committee decides.

Who can join?

Any Muslim who supports HAWA's objects. Ordinary members are 18 or over and can vote and stand for the committee. Associate membership is for under-18s and friends of the community who want to take part without voting.

Is HAWA linked to any political or religious group?

No. HAWA is non-profit, non-political and non-sectarian. It does not promote any ideology, maslak or school of thought.

How is the committee chosen?

At each AGM, members elect the President, Vice-President, Secretary and Treasurer, plus three to five ordinary committee members, for one year. Nominations go to the Secretary at least 14 days before the AGM. If nominations exceed places, there is a ballot, secret if any member asks.

How is HAWA's money handled?

Through HAWA's own bank account, never personal accounts. Three office bearers are authorised signatories and any two must approve each payment. HAWA reports as a Tier 1 association, presents financial statements at each AGM, and members may inspect the financial records on reasonable notice.

Can HAWA help with a funeral or repatriation?

Yes. Supporting families with repatriation, funeral arrangements and burial is one of HAWA's objects, including coordination with the UAIA Burial Society. Use "I need help" and the welfare lead will call.

How do I raise a concern?

Talk to the person first, within 14 days. If that doesn't work, lodge a written complaint with the Secretary. Mediation can follow if both sides agree, then a committee decision with written reasons.
